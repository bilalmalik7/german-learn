Day 15: Was hast du gemacht? The Perfekt with “haben”

Talking about the past: gemacht, gegessen, besucht, studiert

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Build the Perfekt: haben + Partizip II
  • Form participles of regular, irregular and mixed verbs
  • Know the verbs without ge- (-ieren, be-, ver-, er- …)
  • Tell someone what you did yesterday or last weekend

Grammar

Perfekt (spoken past) with “haben”

Ich habe gestern Pizza gegessen.

Summary

  • Perfekt = haben/sein (pos. 2) + Partizip II (end)
  • gemacht · gearbeitet · gesehen · gebracht
  • studiert, besucht, verstanden (no ge-)
  • nicht before the participle
🇩🇪 Culture tip: On Monday mornings, German colleagues typically ask „Na, wie war dein Wochenende?“ – a short honest answer is expected, not just „gut“. Tell one thing you did!
