Day 4: Meine Familie: possessives & saying “no”

mein, dein, sein, ihr … · nicht, kein, doch

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Talk about your family
  • Use possessive articles: mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, Ihr
  • Negate with kein and nicht
  • Answer negative questions with doch

Grammar

Possessive articles (my, your, his …)

mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, ihr/Ihr – endings like ein

Saying no: nein, nicht, kein & doch

kein for nouns without/with ein, nicht for everything else

Summary

  • mein/dein/sein/ihr/unser/euer/ihr/Ihr – endings like ein
  • his = sein, her = ihr
  • kein = not a / no; nicht = not
  • doch = yes, after a negative question
🇩🇪 Culture tip: Family celebrations are important in Germany: on birthdays the person celebrating often brings the cake to work themselves! Saying „Herzlichen Glückwunsch“ before the birthday is considered bad luck.
