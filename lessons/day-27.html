Day 27: Menschen, die ich mag: relative clauses

der Mann, der …, den …, dem … · die Stadt, in der ich wohne

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Build relative clauses with der, die, das, den, dem, denen, dessen, deren
  • Find gender (from the noun) and case (from the role in the clause)
  • Use relative clauses with prepositions (in der, mit dem, auf den)
  • Use was and wo as relative words; describe people

Grammar

Relative clauses (der, die, das, dem, denen …)

Summary

  • der/die/das/die · den · dem/der/dem/denen · dessen/deren
  • Gender from the noun, case from the role in the clause
  • Preposition first: die Stadt, in der …; der Freund, mit dem …
  • was after alles/nichts/etwas/das Beste · wo for places
🇩🇪 Culture tip: Germans value directness in descriptions – but compliments about people’s appearance are rarer than in many cultures. Praising someone’s work or reliability („Du bist so zuverlässig!“) is a big compliment in Germany.
