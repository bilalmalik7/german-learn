Day 2: Woher kommst du? Verbs, questions & numbers

Present tense, verb in position 2, W-questions, 0–1000

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Conjugate any regular verb in the present tense
  • Know the golden rule: the verb is in position 2
  • Ask W-questions and yes/no questions
  • Count from 0 to 1000 and say your age and phone number

Grammar

Present tense of regular verbs

stem + -e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en

Word order: verb in position 2 & questions

The conjugated verb is always the 2nd element in a statement

Numbers 0 – 1,000,000

eins, zwei … einundzwanzig – units before tens!

Summary

  • Endings: -e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en
  • arbeiten → du arbeitest, er arbeitet · heißen → du heißt
  • Verb in position 2 – Heute lerne ich …
  • W-question: W-word + verb + subject · yes/no: verb first
  • 21 = einundzwanzig
🇩🇪 Culture tip: Germans write the number 1 with a long upstroke and often cross the 7 (7̶) so it doesn’t look like a 1. Decimal numbers use a comma: 2,5 = two point five.
