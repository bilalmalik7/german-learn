Day 18: Weil, dass, wenn: connecting sentences

und/aber/denn · deshalb/trotzdem · weil/dass/wenn/ob/als → verb at the end

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Connect sentences with und, aber, oder, denn, sondern (position 0)
  • Use deshalb, trotzdem, dann, außerdem, sonst (position 1 → inversion)
  • Build subordinate clauses with weil, dass, wenn, ob, obwohl, als – verb to the end
  • Distinguish als / wenn / wann and ask indirect questions

Grammar

Connecting sentences: und, aber, denn, deshalb …

Position 0 conjunctions vs adverbs that take position 1

Subordinate clauses: weil, dass, wenn, ob, als …

The conjugated verb goes to the END

Summary

  • Position 0: und, aber, oder, denn, sondern
  • Position 1: deshalb, trotzdem, dann, außerdem, sonst → verb next
  • Verb last: weil, dass, wenn, ob, obwohl, als, bevor, damit …
  • als (once, past) · wenn (if / whenever) · wann (question)
🇩🇪 Culture tip: German sentences can get long – Mark Twain joked that a German sentence is like diving: the writer disappears and comes up on the other side of the Atlantic with the verb in his mouth. Now you know why: subordinate clauses!
