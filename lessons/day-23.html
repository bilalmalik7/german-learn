Day 23: Berlin-Tour: the genitive & n-declension

des Mannes, der Stadt · wegen, trotz, während · den Studenten, den Bären

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Express possession with the genitive: des Mannes, der Frau
  • Use names with -s: Lenas Buch
  • Use wegen, trotz, während, statt
  • Decline n-declension nouns: den Studenten, dem Kollegen, des Bären

Grammar

The genitive case (possession)

des Mannes, der Frau · wegen, trotz, während

n-declension (weak masculine nouns)

der Student → den/dem/des Studenten

🇩🇪 Culture tip: Berlin’s symbol is the bear – it appears on the city’s coat of arms. The Brandenburg Gate (built 1788–1791) stood right next to the Wall and is today the symbol of German reunification. The TV tower (Fernsehturm) at Alexanderplatz is 368 metres high.
