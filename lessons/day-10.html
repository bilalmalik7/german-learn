Day 10: Wir kochen! Negation in detail & the imperative

Where does “nicht” go? · Komm! Kommt! Kommen Sie!

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Place nicht correctly in any sentence
  • Use nichts, niemand, nie, noch nicht, nicht mehr
  • Give instructions with the imperative (du, ihr, Sie)
  • Follow and give a simple recipe

Grammar

Negation in detail: where does “nicht” go?

nicht at the end – but before the 2nd verb part, adjectives and prepositions

The imperative (giving instructions)

Komm! Kommt! Kommen Sie!

Summary

  • nicht at the end – but before 2nd verb part, adjectives, prepositional phrases
  • kein for ein/no article; nichts, niemand, nie, noch nicht, nicht mehr
  • Imperative: Komm! Kommt! Kommen Sie! · Gib! Nimm! Fahr! · Sei! Seid! Seien Sie!
  • Soften with bitte, mal, doch
🇩🇪 Culture tip: A typical German home-cooked dish: Kartoffelsalat (potato salad). In the north it’s made with mayonnaise, in the south with vinegar and broth – and Germans argue passionately about which is better!
