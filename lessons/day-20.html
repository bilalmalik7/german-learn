Day 20: Einkaufen: adjective endings after der, die, das

der rote Pullover, den roten Pullover · dieser, welcher

▶ Start this lesson (with audio)

What you learn today

  • Know when adjectives have endings (before a noun) and when not
  • Use the endings after der/die/das, dieser, welcher: only -e or -en
  • Talk about clothes, colours and sizes
  • Shop for clothes: try on, ask for sizes, compare

Grammar

Adjective endings 1: after der, die, das

Only -e or -en: the five -e endings

Summary

  • Before a noun: ending · after sein: no ending
  • After der/die/das/dieser/welcher: -e in Nom. sg. + Akk. f/n; otherwise -en
  • der rote / den roten / dem roten / die roten
  • Das steht dir! · Kann ich … anprobieren?
🇩🇪 Culture tip: German clothing sizes differ from UK/US sizes: a women’s UK 10 is a German 38, a men’s M is often 48–50. Shops let you try things on (anprobieren) in the „Umkleidekabine“ – and most allow returns within 14 days with the receipt.
