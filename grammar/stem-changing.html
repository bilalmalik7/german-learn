Verbs with a vowel change (e → i, e → ie, a → ä)

Verben mit Vokalwechsel – Only du and er/sie/es change: du sprichst, er fährt

🔊 Open with audio & exercisesLesson day 6

Some common verbs change their stem vowel – but only in the du and er/sie/es forms. The endings stay regular.

e → i: sprechene → ie: lesena → ä: fahren
