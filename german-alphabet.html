Vowels can be short or long. As a rule of thumb, a vowel is long before one consonant or an h (Tag, gehen, Sohn) and short before a double consonant (Bett, Mutter, kommen).

Ä, Ö, Ü and ß – the extra letters

The two dots on ä, ö, ü are called Umlaut. They change the sound and often the meaning: schon (already) vs schön (beautiful), Bruder (brother) vs Brüder (brothers).

  • Ä is close to the “e” in bed: Männer, Käse.
  • Ö: say “e” (as in say) and round your lips: schön, Öl.
  • Ü: say “ee” and round your lips as if for “oo”: über, müde, Tür.
  • ß is a sharp “s”. It comes after a long vowel or a diphthong (Straße, heißen); after a short vowel you write ss (Wasser, muss).

No German keyboard? Write ae, oe, ue and ss: Mueller, Strasse.

Memory trick for ei and ie: say the second letter in English. ei → “i” = “eye” (drei), ie → “e” = “ee” (vier). So Wein (wine) and Wien (Vienna) sound different.

Spelling on the phone: A wie Anton

At the doctor's, at the bank or on the phone you will often hear Können Sie das bitte buchstabieren? (Could you spell that, please?). Germans then use a spelling alphabet: each letter gets a word, and you say “A wie Anton” (A as in Anton). This is the traditional list from the German standard DIN 5009, which most people know:

Example – spelling the name Müller: “M wie Martha, Ü wie Übermut, zweimal L wie Ludwig, E wie Emil, R wie Richard.” For double letters say zweimal (twice) or Doppel-L.

Since 2022 DIN 5009 officially uses a new list based on German city names (for example A wie Aachen, B wie Berlin). The traditional name list above is still what you will hear most often.

FAQ: the German alphabet

How many letters does the German alphabet have?

German uses the same 26 letters as English plus the three umlauts Ä, Ö and Ü and the letter ß (Eszett). That makes 30 letters, although the umlauts and ß are often not counted as separate letters of the alphabet.

How do I type Ä, Ö, Ü and ß without a German keyboard?

On phones, press and hold A, O, U or S to see the special letters. If you really cannot type them, write ae, oe, ue and ss instead: Mueller for Müller, Strasse for Straße. This is understood everywhere, but use the real letters in exams and official forms.

When do I write ß and when ss?

Write ß after a long vowel or a diphthong (Straße, groß, heißen) and ss after a short vowel (Wasser, muss, Klasse). In Switzerland ß is not used at all. In capital letters people usually write SS, although a capital ẞ also exists.

What is “A wie Anton”?

It is how Germans spell words on the phone: every letter gets a name, A wie Anton, B wie Berta, C wie Cäsar and so on. The traditional list comes from the German standard DIN 5009. Since 2022 the standard uses city names instead, but the old names are still widely used.

Is German pronunciation hard for English speakers?

Most of it is easy because German is so regular. The sounds that need practice are ü, ö, the two ch sounds and the German r. Listen and repeat a few minutes every day, for example with the pronunciation trainer and the spelling drills in the listening gym.
