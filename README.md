# 🇩🇪 Deutsch in 30 Tagen – Learn German step by step

An animated, beginner-friendly website that takes you from your first „Hallo“ to relative clauses, the passive and Konjunktiv II in **30 days of 2–3 hours each**.

## How to run it

### As a public website (current setup)

The website is **open for everyone**: no login, no teacher portal, all 30 days, all scenes and Job German open.
Progress is saved in each visitor’s browser (Settings → Export / Import moves it to another device).
This is controlled by `PUBLIC = true` in `js/standalone.js`; set it to `false` to bring back the logins and the
teacher portal described below (the server code in `api/v1.js` is kept). Anyone who was logged in keeps their
progress – it is moved into normal browser storage automatically.

### With logins and teacher portal (switched off)

The repository is ready for **Vercel** (free plan): static pages plus one small server function, `api/v1.js`.
It adds real logins – the teacher creates a username and password for each student in the teacher portal – and
stores every student’s progress, activity, messages and a private backup on the server.

1. Import the repository in Vercel (no framework, no build command).
2. *Storage → Create → Blob* (access **private**) and connect it to the project (sets `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`).
3. Environment variables:
   - `SESSION_SECRET` – a long random string (signs the logins)
   - `TEACHER_USERNAME` – optional, default `teacher`
   - `TEACHER_PASSWORD` – the teacher’s first password (can be changed later on *👤 Account*)
   - `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` – optional; turns on the AI features (writing analysis, AI conversations, AI tutor)
4. Deploy, open the site, log in as the teacher → **📊 Teacher portal → ➕ Add students**.

Saving is deliberately frugal (routine progress is sent every 10 minutes and when the page is closed; messages,
registrations and teacher actions immediately), so 5–10 students fit comfortably into the free Blob allowance.

### Plans & payments (website version) – currently switched off

The course is **free for everyone** right now (`ENABLED = false` in `js/plans.js`). Set it to `true` to bring back
the plans, the free-trial limits and the payment tools described below.


Three monthly plans, defined in `js/plans.js` (names, prices, features, which pages they open):

| Plan | Price | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 Basic | Rs 5,000 | 30 daily lessons, grammar A–Z, trainer, review, sounds |
| 🚀 Standard | Rs 7,000 | + real-life scenes, speaking practice, writing studio |
| 👑 Plus | Rs 10,000 | + messages/corrections from the teacher, AI tutor and AI writing analysis |

The teacher picks a plan when creating a login and records payments in the student’s detail view (each payment
extends the plan by one month). Students see their plan and the teacher’s payment instructions on **💳 Plans**.

**Free trial:** visitors without a login and students without a plan can browse the course (30-day plan, scenes list,
grammar index, writing tasks) but only *play* **Day 1** and the scene **“Im Supermarkt”**; everything else shows a lock with
the WhatsApp contact (+49 155 67783533, set in `js/plans.js`).
Payments themselves happen outside the site (cash, bank transfer, JazzCash / EasyPaisa).

### 💼 Job German – Sales Advisor (open for everyone)

Prepared for a part-time Sales Advisor job in a fashion store (e.g. H&M Osnabrück): how the application works,
16 typical interview questions with model answers (listen + speak), questions to ask, rescue phrases, tips –
and **15 days in the job**, each with 10 key phrases and an animated scene where the learner is the new Sales Advisor
(first day, greeting customers, directions, sizes, fitting room, styling, backstage, till, returns, complaints,
pick-ups & phone, shift plan, Saturday rush, sale & recycling, feedback & closing). Plus an interview scene.

### 🎓 Exam prep – telc & Goethe, A1 → B1 in 12 weeks (`#/exam`)

- **Roadmap:** 12 weeks, about 2–3 hours a day, Monday to Saturday (Sunday: review). Each week has can-do goals,
  a plan per day that links the 30 course days, grammar topics, exam words, notes sheets, scenes and exam tasks,
  and checkboxes. Course days, scenes and exam tasks tick themselves when they are done. Week 4 = A1 mock,
  week 8 = A2 mock, week 12 = B1 mocks. An optional exam date shows a countdown.
- **Formats:** Goethe and telc side by side for A1, A2 and B1 (parts, timing, points, pass marks) with strategies.
- **Practice tests:** 49 tasks covering every part of Start Deutsch 1 (Goethe & telc A1), Goethe A2, Goethe B1
  and telc B1 (Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, Sprechen, telc Sprachbausteine). Listening is read by the browser’s voices
  and can be played only as often as in the exam; the transcript appears after checking. Every answer has an
  English explanation; a/b/c options appear in a fixed shuffled order. Writing tasks have a word counter, content
  points, a quick check, useful phrases and a model answer; speaking tasks have cards, a preparation timer and a
  model answer to listen to and repeat (with speech scoring).
- **Mock exams:** timed parts (e.g. Goethe B1: Lesen 65 min, Hören 40 min, Schreiben 60 min) that submit themselves
  when the time is up, a score per part against the 60 % pass mark, and a results history.
- **Exam words & notes:** 18 topics (A1–B1) with article, plural and English, a quiz, irregular verbs, verbs with
  prepositions, connectors by word order, phrases for writing and speaking, and 12 one-page notes sheets (printable).

All practice texts are original – written for this course in the official task formats, not copied from exam
papers. Exam formats follow the descriptions by Goethe-Institut and telc (2025/26); learners should check
goethe.de / telc.net before their exam and also do the free official model tests (linked in the app).
Progress is saved in `Store.state.exam` and travels with the progress link.

### As a plain static copy

Without the server function everything still works except logins, the teacher portal and AI.

- **Easiest:** open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge.
- **Best (microphone works reliably):** serve it over http(s), e.g.
  ```bash
  python3 -m http.server 8000
  # then open http://localhost:8000
  ```
- **Online:** enable *GitHub Pages* for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from branch, root folder).

### Audio & microphone

- Audio uses your device’s built-in German text-to-speech voice. For the most natural sound use Chrome (“Google Deutsch”), Edge (“Microsoft Katja/Conrad Online”) or Safari (“Anna”). If you hear no German voice, add German in your operating system’s speech settings. You can choose the voice and speed in ⚙️ Settings.
- Pronunciation scoring uses the browser’s speech recognition (Chrome / Edge, internet connection needed). Allow microphone access when asked.

### Your progress

Progress is stored in your browser (`localStorage`, one copy per login). With a login it is also backed up to the server and restored automatically on a new device. Without a login use **Settings → Export / Import** to move it.

### Adding or editing content

Each day in `js/data/weekN.js` is a plain object: `vocab` entries are `[german, forms, english, example, exampleEnglish]`; exercises are `{ t: 'mc' | 'fill' | 'order' | 'tr' | 'listen', … }`. In grammar HTML, `{{Deutsch|English}}` renders an example with an audio button and `[[Deutsch]]` makes a word clickable.

## A note on expectations

30 days × 2–3 hours ≈ 75–90 hours of focused study. That builds a solid **A1–A2 foundation and introduces every core B1 grammar structure** – you’ll understand how German sentences work and handle everyday conversations. Perfect, automatic grammar in fast speech comes from continued daily practice after the 30 days: keep using the review section, find a tandem partner and keep speaking.
